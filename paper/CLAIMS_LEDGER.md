not upgraded merely because its number can be regenerated. The evidence type,
scope, and counter-observation must also match the sentence being claimed.

## How to use it

The paper may retain a headline only when the current disposition is explicit in
the sentence or its nearby limitations. A result marked `scenario only`,
`conditional`, or `existence observation` must not be described as a measured,
general, peer-reviewed finding.
