# Research product demo capture plan

Status: reference preparation only.

Target length: 45 to 60 seconds.

1. Open `https://test.autoyou.me/` and hold on the public entry screen.
2. Explain that the public surface starts with account and plan selection.
3. Show the capability-routing figure: ordinary extraction, RAG, summary and
   simple code can be routed to the edge; hard reasoning stays on the cloud
   path.
4. Show the savings-by-baseline figure and call out the Gemini counterexample.
   Do not present edge inference as universally better.
5. Show the adaptation figure: at alpha 0.70 the quality gain is positive,
   while coverage is unchanged.
6. End on the measured-energy figure and the repository reproduction command.

Capture rules:

- Do not sign in, choose a paid plan, or enter credentials during the public
  reference capture.
- Use synthetic prompts only if an interactive local run is added later.
- Keep the caveats visible: the workload mix is an assumption, and the
  measurements are bounded to the published hardware and workload.
